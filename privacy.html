Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-19

1. Overview

Imagix is a free online image toolkit. The core tools (resize, compress, convert, crop, rotate, watermark, base64, filter) run entirely in your web browser. Your images are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. We do not operate accounts, and we do not ask you to sign up.

2. Information we collect automatically

When you visit Imagix, third-party services (such as Google AdSense and Amazon Associates) may automatically collect certain technical information through cookies, web beacons (also called pixel tags or clear GIFs), and server logs. This may include:

  • Your IP address and approximate location (country / region).
  • Browser type, device type, operating system, and screen resolution.
  • Pages viewed, referring URLs, and interaction events (for analytics and ad measurement).
  • Cookies and similar identifiers set by our advertising and affiliate partners.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

We and our partners use third-party cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Measure and analyze site traffic (analytics).
  • Show relevant advertisements (Google AdSense) and measure their performance.
  • Attribute qualifying purchases made through Amazon affiliate links (Amazon Associates).

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how ads and some features behave.

4. Advertising and affiliate partners

Imagix may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by the Google Ads settings and the Google privacy policy.

Imagix participates in the Amazon Associates program. As an Amazon Associate, Imagix earns from qualifying purchases. Links to Amazon are tagged and may set cookies to track referrals.

Learn more about how Google uses data when you use our site and our partners' sites: https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/

5. Your images

Because all image processing happens in your browser, the photos and files you open are not transmitted to Imagix. We never see, store, or share the content of your images.

6. Children's privacy

Imagix is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site operator via the information provided on the homepage.
