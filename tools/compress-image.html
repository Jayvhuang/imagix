Frequently asked questions

常见问题

Does compressing reduce quality?

JPEG and WebP are lossy at lower quality settings; PNG is re-encoded losslessly. Use a higher quality value to keep more detail.

Why is my PNG not getting smaller?

PNG is already lossless; for large savings convert to JPEG or WebP instead.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Compression runs locally in your browser and the file never leaves your device.

压缩会降低画质吗？

JPEG 与 WebP 在较低质量下为有损；PNG 为无损重编码。提高质量值可保留更多细节。

为什么我的 PNG 没有变小？

PNG 本身已是无损；要大幅减小体积，请改用 JPEG 或 WebP。

我的图片会被上传吗？

不会。压缩在浏览器本地完成，文件不会离开你的设备。
