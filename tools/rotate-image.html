Frequently asked questions

常见问题

Does rotating change resolution?

Rotating 90° swaps width and height; the total pixel count is unchanged.

Can I undo a rotation?

Yes — use Reset to restore the original, or apply another rotate/flip.

Are EXIF orientations fixed?

Images are loaded with their EXIF orientation applied, so they appear upright.

旋转会改变分辨率吗？

旋转 90° 会交换宽高；总像素数不变。

可以撤销旋转吗？

可以——使用“重置”还原原图，或再次旋转 / 翻转。

EXIF 方向会被修正吗？

图片加载时已应用 EXIF 方向，因此会正立显示。
