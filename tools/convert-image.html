Frequently asked questions

常见问题

Which formats are supported?

You can convert to PNG, JPEG or WebP.

Does WebP keep transparency?

Yes, WebP supports transparency, like PNG.

Will converting change colors?

Converting to JPEG drops transparency (replaced by a background) and is lossy; PNG and WebP preserve quality better.

支持哪些格式？

可转换为 PNG、JPEG 或 WebP。

WebP 支持透明吗？

是的，WebP 与 PNG 一样支持透明。

转换会改变颜色吗？

转为 JPEG 会丢失透明（以背景替代）且有损；PNG 与 WebP 更好地保留画质。
